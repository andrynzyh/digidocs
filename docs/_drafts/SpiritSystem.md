### Books

- You can obtain <img src="/img/ico/Bokomons_Book_of_Knowledge.png"/> Bokomon's Book of Knowledge items from the Cash Shop, from the NPC Digitamamon (Item Make), located in DATS Center or Kaiser/Magna Box.
- The requirements for creation are 3 different Bokomon's Page of Knowledge and 1 s <img src="/img/ico/RPTera.png"/>
- You can collect these pages from defeating KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon in Server Continent Pyramid (there are many).
- Alternatively you can work together with other players to achieve the goal of defeating 200 mobs to summon several Susanoomon on the midle of map.
- Kaiser & Magna Box have chance to drop <img src="/img/ico/Bokomons_Book_of_Knowledge.png"/> Bokomon's Book of Knowledge items


## Susanoomon
- You need all the Spirit of Shining items to make KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon
- Spirit of Shining items can be obtained from Bokomon (NPC) at Dats Center.
- In order to make one, you need have a Spirit Digimon of level 100 to sacrifice.

```
Susanoomon Quests Requirements
- Lv90+ Tamer.
- Lv99 KaiserGreymon.
- Lv99 MagnaGarurumon.
```
**You pick up their quests at Bokomon (NPC) at DATS Center**

KaiserGreymon Quests

```
1. Talk to Bokomon (NPC) at Dats Center.
2. Talk to Angelwomon (NPC) at Silver Lake.
3. Kill the Cherubimon boss at Silver Lake once again. (Near the Kunemon and Dokunemon spot)
4. Kill the Dynasmon boss at Lost Historic Site. (Near the Musyamon Captain spot)
```

MagnaGarurumon Quests

```
1. Talk to the Bokomon (NPC) at Dats Center.
2. Talk to the Seraphimon NPC at Distorted Data Village. (Check the NPC location in your map)
3. Kill the ShadowSeraphimon boss at Distorted Data Village once more. (Near the lava lake area, next to MetalEtemon)
4. Kill the LordKnightmon boss at Ruined Historic area.
5. Kill the Seraphimon boss at Distorted Data Village.
```

## Susanoomon (Shin)

Same as Susanoomon guide but you need to level 120 and take to diffrent NPC
- If you get a Spirit Digimon to level 120, you can take it to Susanoomon (Shin) (NPC) to craft a Spirit of Lunar/Solar item (the Digimon will be permanently deleted in the process).
- You need 20 <img src="/img/ico/Bokomons_Book_of_Knowledge.png"/> Boko-Books in total, you will need additional 20T if you're crafting them with the <img src="/img/ico/Stained_Page_of_Knowledge.png"/> Boko-Pages.
- Normal Spirits and Hyper Spirits are "hatched" as 3/5 Digimon.
