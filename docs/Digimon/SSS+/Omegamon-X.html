Quest Guide

Requirements

  • Agumon (Classic) Lv99
  • Omegamon unlocked
  • WarGreymon X unlocked
  • Tamer Lv99

Steps

  1. Defeat 5 Dorumon, Dracmon, Kunemon and FanBeemon.
  2. Collect 10 Yggdrasil's Records.
    Consumed

    10 Records are consumed by the quest.

    Tip

    They are returned after changing channel.

Notes

Character Bound

X-Antibody Factor Omegamon Alpha is Character Bound.

Quest Requirement

Although the quest only asks for 10 of each quest item, you need 30 in total because 20 more are required for crafting.

Yggdrasil's Records

10 Records consumed by the quest are returned after changing channel.
