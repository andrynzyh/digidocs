Spirit System

About this Guide

This guide covers the complete Spirit System progression, from obtaining your first Spirit Digimon to crafting Cosmic Susanoomon (Shin).

If future game updates change recipes or quest requirements, this page will be updated accordingly.

Overview

The Spirit System allows players to collect the Ten Legendary Warrior Spirits, evolve them into KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon, and eventually unlock Susanoomon, Susanoomon (Shin), and Cosmic Susanoomon.

Overview​

The Spirit System is one of the longest progression systems in DMW.

Throughout this guide, you'll learn:

  • How to obtain Spirit Digimon
  • How to craft Bokomon's Book of Knowledge
  • How to evolve KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon
  • How to unlock Susanoomon
  • How to upgrade to Susanoomon (Shin)
  • How to obtain Cosmic Susanoomon

Progression​

Follow the Spirit System in this order:

  • ✅ Collect the Ten Legendary Warrior Spirits
  • ✅ Craft Spirit of Shining
  • ✅ Unlock KaiserGreymon
  • ✅ Unlock MagnaGarurumon
  • ✅ Complete the Susanoomon questline
  • ✅ Upgrade to Susanoomon (Shin)
  • ✅ Craft Cosmic Susanoomon (Shin)

Progression

Each stage unlocks the next. You cannot skip directly to a higher Spirit evolution.

Spirit Attributes​

Each Legendary Warrior Spirit represents one of the ten elemental attributes.

Spirit items can be obtained by defeating Digimon that share the corresponding attribute.

Attribute Farming

For example, Fire attribute Digimon can drop materials related to the Fire Spirit, while Water attribute Digimon can drop materials related to the Water Spirit.

Bokomon's Book of Knowledge​

Bokomon's Book of Knowledge is a core material required for Spirit evolution and crafting.

Ways to Obtain​

  • 🛒 Purchase from the Cash Shop
  • 🔨 Craft through Digitamamon (Item Craft) in DATS Center
  • 📦 Obtain from Kaiser & Magna Box

Crafting Requirements​

To craft one Bokomon's Book of Knowledge, you need:

  • 3 different Bokomon's Pages of Knowledge
  • 1 RP Tera
How to Collect Bokomon Pages

Bokomon Pages can be obtained from:

  • Defeating KaiserGreymon
  • Defeating MagnaGarurumon
  • Defeating the summoned Susanoomon
  • Opening Kaiser & Magna Boxes

Susanoomon​

Susanoomon is created by combining KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon.

Requirements​

  • Tamer Level 90+
  • Lv.99 KaiserGreymon
  • Lv.99 MagnaGarurumon
Quest Location

All Susanoomon-related quests begin with Bokomon in DATS Center.

Evolution Requirements​

To evolve into Susanoomon, you must first obtain both KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon.

Quest Start

Speak with Bokomon in DATS Center to begin the Susanoomon questline.

Quest guide​

🔥 KaiserGreymon Quest
  1. Talk to Bokomon in DATS Center.
  2. Talk to Angewomon in Silver Lake.
  3. Defeat Cherubimon in Silver Lake.
    • Located near the Kunemon and Dokunemon area.
  4. Defeat Dynasmon in the Lost Historic Site.
    • Located near the Musyamon Captain area.
❄️ MagnaGarurumon Quest
  1. Talk to Bokomon in DATS Center.
  2. Talk to Seraphimon in Distorted Data Village.
  3. Defeat ShadowSeraphimon.
    • Located near the lava lake beside MetalEtemon.
  4. Defeat LordKnightmon in the Ruined Historic Site.
  5. Defeat Seraphimon in Distorted Data Village.
Before You Start

Make sure both KaiserGreymon and MagnaGarurumon are at Level 99 before starting the Susanoomon questline.

Susanoomon (Shin)​

Susanoomon (Shin) is the upgraded version of Susanoomon. Instead of combining Hyper Spirits, you must sacrifice Level 120 Spirit Digimon to craft new evolution materials.

Spirit Consumption

Spirit Digimon used during crafting are permanently deleted.

Requirements​

Before crafting Susanoomon (Shin), make sure you have:

  • ✅ Spirit Digimon at Level 120
  • ✅ Access to the Susanoomon (Shin) NPC
  • ✅ Bokomon's Book of Knowledge
  • ✅ Required crafting materials

Crafting Process​

  1. Level a Spirit Digimon to Lv.120.
  2. Visit the Susanoomon (Shin) NPC.
  3. Sacrifice the Spirit Digimon.
  4. Receive either:
    • 🌞 Spirit of Solar
    • 🌙 Spirit of Lunar
  5. Repeat until all required materials have been collected.

Required Materials​

  • 20 × Bokomon's Book of Knowledge
  • 20 Tera (when crafting books from Bokomon Pages)
  • Multiple Spirit of Solar and Spirit of Lunar materials
Crafting Tip

Normal Spirits and Hyper Spirits are created as 3/5 Digimon, so you don't need to worry about hatch size before sacrificing them.

Frequently Asked Questions​

Do Spirit Digimon need to be Level 120?

Only when crafting Susanoomon (Shin) materials.

Can I buy Bokomon's Book of Knowledge?

Yes. It can be obtained through crafting, certain boxes, and player trading.

Are Spirit Digimon consumed during crafting?

Yes. Any Spirit Digimon used during the crafting process is permanently deleted.

Can I skip directly to Susanoomon (Shin)?

No. You must complete each stage of the Spirit progression in order.
