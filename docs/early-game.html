Early Game Core Progression

Now that you've obtained your 30-Day Underworld Gear, it's time to start replacing it with permanent equipment before it expires.

Complete the Main Quest​

Your first goal is to finish the main questline in every major region.

Completing these quests unlocks important game features, rewards, and dungeon tickets.

Recommended order:

  • File Island
  • Server Continent
  • Tokyo-Odaiba
  • Spiral Mountain

Choose Your Playstyle​

Decide which role you want to focus on.

  • ⚔️ Auto Attack (AA)
  • ✨ Skill Damage (SK)
  • 🛡️ Tank (TA)

See the class guide for the differences between each playstyle.

Build Your Team​

Prepare at least one Digimon for each attribute.

  • Vaccine (VA)
  • Data (DA)
  • Virus (VI)

This allows you to deal bonus damage against every enemy type.

Skill Damage (SK)​

Skill DPS is not recommended for beginners.

Most strong SK Digimon are expensive, and the class relies heavily on good gear before it performs well.

Increase Your Power​

There are four main systems that improve your character.

Clone Your Digimon​

Visit Richard Sampson at DATS Center and clone your Digimon.

Aim for Perfect Clone 15 on:

  • AT
  • CT
  • BL

If your Digimon is Transcended, also clone:

  • HP

Upgrade Your Accessories​

Accessories provide a large amount of your total stats.

New players receive a 30-Day Underworld Accessory Set, which is strong enough for early game.

When you're ready to upgrade:

Auto Attack​

  • Radiant Qinglongmon Ring
  • Radiant Qinglongmon Necklace
  • Radiant Qinglongmon Earring

Tank​

  • Shiny Xuanwumon Ring
  • Shiny Xuanwumon Necklace
  • Radiant Qinglongmon Earring

Unlock Better Decks​

Deck Buffs provide a significant increase in damage.

Auto Attack​

  1. Main Characters of Adventure
  2. Burst Ultimate Wings
  3. Corrupted Power

Tank​

  1. Brick Wall
  2. Defensive Order
  3. Dragon Scale

Skill Damage​

  1. Cosmic Spirit Evolution
  2. Latent Power

Collect Seals​

Start collecting seals as early as possible.

Many common seals are inexpensive and provide permanent stat increases.

Recommended milestone:

  • 1
  • 50
  • 200
  • 500
  • 1,000
  • 3,000

For beginners, try to reach 500 seals for:

  • AT
  • HT
  • HP

You can upgrade them further later in the game.

Before Moving to Mid Game​

Make sure you have:

Accessories​

  • Semi-Perfect Radiant Holy Beast Lv.5 Set
  • Royal : X-Knight Bracelet

Deck Buffs​

PlaystyleRecommended Deck
Auto AttackOur Final Evolution
SkillForsaken Strain

Seals​

Upgrade most overworld-farmable seals to:

  • 500 (minimum)
  • 3,000 (recommended if possible)

Once you've completed everything above, you're ready to progress into the Mid Game.
