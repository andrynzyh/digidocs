D-Ark Digivice

The D-Ark Digivice is a progression-based Digivice that provides permanent stat bonuses and unique visual effects for your Tamer.

Each version grants higher stats than the previous one, making it an important upgrade throughout the game.

You can craft and upgrade D-Ark Digivices through Jijimon (Guide) in DMW Village.

info

Getting Started​

There are two ways to obtain D-Ark Version 1:

  • Complete all Shinjuku quests.
  • Craft it using a Ticket to D-Ark Digivice [Level 1], which drops from Infinite Mountain Dungeon 2.0.

D-Ark Stats​

Unlike True Digivices, D-Ark stat bonuses are fixed and cannot be rerolled.

Each D-Ark provides:

  • HP
  • Attack
  • Defense

These stat bonuses apply to both your Digimon and your Tamer, effectively providing approximately 1.5×–1.65× the listed values.

For example:

tip

A D-Ark Version 5 can provide up to 825 Attack to your Digimon after the bonus multiplier.

Crafting D-Ark Digivice

All D-Ark upgrades are crafted through Jijimon (Guide) in DMW Village.

info

Every upgrade has a 100% Success Rate.
