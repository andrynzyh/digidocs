Spiral KeyRing

Spiral KeyRings are endgame accessories designed for specific playstyles. There are three different variants:

  • ⚔️ Auto Attack (AA)
  • ✨ Skill (SK)
  • 🛡️ Tank (TA)

Each variant provides unique fixed stats that cannot be changed.

KeyRings are crafted and upgraded through Taichi (Spiral Mountain) at DATS Center.

info

Materials are obtained from:

  • Spiral Mountain
  • Marine Dragon Domain
  • Front Yard of Marionette Mansion
  • Back of the Empire
  • Stage of Clown

Crafting Spiral KeyRings

The crafting progression is identical for AA, SK, and Tank KeyRings.

Simply replace the previous level with the same variant.

info
  • NPC: Taichi (Spiral Mountain)
  • Location: DATS Center
  • Success Rate: 100%
