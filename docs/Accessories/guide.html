Accessory Enhancement Guide

Most accessories (Ring, Necklace, Earring, Bracelet, and some Digivices) can be upgraded using the following items.

Stone​

Option Change Stone (OCS)​

Re-rolls the stat options on an accessory.

Features

  • Changes the accessory's available stats.
  • Up to 3 options can be locked.
  • Locking more options increases the OCS cost.

Number Change Stone (NCS)​

Re-rolls the value of an existing stat.

DMW Quality of Life

  • Stat values will never decrease.
  • A re-roll is not guaranteed to increase the value.

Digitary Power Stone​

Increases an accessory's Digitary Power.

Digitary Power determines how much of each stat the accessory provides.

Maximum Digitary Power: 200%

Types

ItemEffect
Digitary Power Stone+1% on success / -1% on failure
Shiny Digitary Power Stone+3% on success / -2% on failure
Amazing Digitary Power StoneGuaranteed +10%

Digiable Power Renewal Number​

Using OCS or NCS consumes Digiable Power Renewal Numbers.

1 OCS/NCS = 1 Renewal Number

Example:

  • Option reroll costs 91 OCS
  • You must have 91 Digiable Power Renewal Numbers

Increasing Renewal Numbers

Use Renewal Increase Stones.

ItemEffect
Renewal Increase StoneChance to gain +1
High Rank Renewal Increase StoneGuaranteed +1
Amazing Renewal Increase StoneGuaranteed +5

Where to Obtain​

QueenChessmon Shop (DATS)​

Available for purchase:

  • Digitary Power Stone
  • Shiny Digitary Power Stone
  • Renewal Increase Stone

Dungeon & Colosseum​

Obtain from dungeon rewards or Colosseum Exchange:

  • Amazing Digitary Power Stone
  • Amazing Renewal Increase Stone

Special Accessories​

Some accessories have fixed stats and cannot be customized normally.

Examples include:

  • Spiral KeyRing
  • D-Ark Series

These accessories may have:

  • Fixed stat options
  • Fixed stat values
  • Fixed Digitary Power
  • 0 Digiable Power Renewal Numbers when crafted or obtained

Recommended Accessory Options​

Every class has different ideal stat options.

tip

Don't aim for Perfect accessories too early.

A Semi-Perfect accessory (3 out of 4 desired options) is much more resource-efficient until you reach:

  • Lv.10 Fanglongmon Gear
  • Ultimate Fanglongmon Gear
  • Royal : X-Knight Bracelet
