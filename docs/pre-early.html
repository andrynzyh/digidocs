Pre-Early

You'll begin the game by controlling Omegamon X in a short battle against Dexmon. This teaches the basic combat system.

After the battle, complete the tutorial quests until you arrive at DMW Village.

Important​

Keep these items.

  • Journey Exchange Ticket
  • Starter Package Ticket

Do not use them yet.

warning

Ignore the A New World quest for now.

New players cannot finish it yet.

Continue following the tutorial until Teacher Mori sends you back to DMW Village.

Preparing Your Account​

Before leaving DMW Village, do these first.

Start Beginner Challenges​

Accept quests from:

  • Koromon
  • Tsunomon

These quests give permanent progression rewards. you can see quest Here

Exchange Tickets​

Exchange your:

  • Starter Exchange Ticket
  • Journey Exchange Ticket

Choose the package that matches your class.

AA

  • LordKnightmon X

SK

  • Dukemon Shin

Open Packages​

Open only:

  • Starter Package
  • Journey Package
  • Lala's Gift

Save some items for later.

Examples:

  • Underworld Accessories
  • Jogress Chip
  • Raremon Eggs

These are more useful later.

Strengthen Your Digimon​

Go to DATS Center.

Upgrade Starter​

  • Clone every available stat
  • Use Growth Fruit
  • Level to 70
  • Unlock SSS

Upgrade Journey Digimon​

  • Hatch Lv.5 Egg
  • Unlock evolutions
  • Level to 70
  • Clone stats

Equip Digivice​

Equip your free DMW Digivice.

Don't worry about perfect options yet.

We'll upgrade it later.

STARTING THE Novice/SUPER PROGRESSION​

The Novice/Super Jumping system is designed to help new Tamers catch upsignificantly faster. After creating a new Tamer: Reach Tamer Level 2Once you reach Level 2, you will receive mail containing a Good starting Pack. Do not ignore this package. It contains several useful progression items, including tickets that can be exchanged for Digimon andaccessories intended to help you begin playing the diferent roles.

Important Note: super jumping is over However, those who still have the Jumping Mark are still able to complete the quests,

Questing​

Return to DMW Village.

Accept:

  • Elecmon Quest
  • Miki's Summons
  • Commander's Urgent Summon

These unlock the three main questlines.

  • File Island
  • Glacier Area
  • Digimon Maze

Focus on:

  • Main Quests
  • High EXP quests

Sub quests are only needed when progression stops.

MAJOR GOAL​

While questing, don't forget to:

  • Collect Digimon Seals
  • Unlock useful Deck Buffs
  • Upgrade your Starter

Learn more:

  • Digimon Seals
  • Deck System

UNLOCK MAP​

New players should not spend all their time farming one area. Progress through the main missions and unlock your maps. Map progression is important because later areas contain: Dungeons Tours Questlines NPC Exchanges Progression Materials Gear Upgrades useful progression route to keep working through is:

  • File Island → Server Continent → Tokyo → Spiral Mountain
  • You should also progress through Shinjuku → Digital Area → Forest Area. because Forest Area is important for another useful beginner activity explained later in this guide.

LEVEL 120​

After establishing your Jumping progression, work on leveling your Tamer and Digimon.

A strong early target is:

  • Tamer Level 120
  • Digimon Level 120

If you reach around Level 100 and leveling begins slowing down, look for other players farming on the samemap.

Joining a farming party can significantly improve your leveling speed.

Level 120 Digivice Dailies After reaching Level 120, you will have access to two daily missions through your Digivice that can be used to help boost Digimon to Level 120.

Reminder

Remember to switch to the Digimon you want to level BEFORE claiming the reward.

DAILY PRIORITIES​

Once you have started progressing, build a simple daily routine. Claim Daily TicketsPress: P → Claim Daily TicketsDo this every day.

Daily dungeon/content tickets accumulate into future progression opportunities, so forgetting them repeatedly will slow your account down over time. Complete Useful Daily Missions Do the daily content relevant to your current progression. You do not need to complete every activity in DMW immediately. Prioritize content that advances your: Digimon → Gear → Deck → Seals → Vice → Dungeon progression

INVENTORY & WAREHOUSE​

Do not immediately assume you need to spend money for every inventory upgrade.

Events frequently provide opportunities to obtain:

  • Inventory Expansion
  • Warehouse Expansion
  • Archive Expansion

Keep an eye on active events and their rewards.

Inventory space becomes increasingly important as you accumulate dungeon materials, tickets, seals, evolution items and equipment.

DUNGEON PROGRESSION​

After finishing the important Jumping progression and establishing your Digimon, role and basic equipment, you can begin moving deeper into DMW's dungeon progression. The available DMW dungeon guides cover progression content including:

  • Vajra
  • Vika
  • Chaotic
  • Burning
  • Final
  • MDG
  • PDG
  • Mugen
  • Pied
  • Pillars
  • APO
  • Odaiba Park Extreme

Do not interpret this simply as: “I unlocked it, therefore I am ready for it.”

Before progressing into harder content, improve your Digimon, cloning, Deck, Seals, accessories and role-specific equipment. Mechanics also become increasingly important. A weaker player who understands the dungeon can often contribute more effectively than a stronger playerwho ignores mechanics.
