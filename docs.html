DMW — NEW PLAYER & JUMPING / NOVICE/ PROGRESSION GUIDE​

From a Fresh Tamer to Your First Proper Build Welcome to DMW! Starting on DMW can feel overwhelming because there are many systems, Digimon, questlines, dungeons and equipment upgrades available. This guide is designed to give new players a clear progression path:

Create Tamer → Novice/ Jumping → Choose Your Role → Build Your Digimon → Unlock Maps → Improve Gear → Complete Daily Content → Begin Dungeon Progression

You do not need to complete everything immediately. DMW progression is much easier when you work toward one goal at a time.

info

Why choosing a role matters. Do not blindly upgrade every piece of equipment you receive.

Your chosen role affects:

Digimon → Accessory Stats → Vice → Blessing → Gear Progression

The Super Jumping system helps considerably because it provides equipment and Digimon options thatallow a new player to begin building toward these roles. If you are unsure which role to play, ask experienced players before spending valuable resources or making an important ticket selection.

UNDERSTANDING DMW ROLES​

One of the biggest differences between Digital Masters World (DMW) and traditional DMO servers is the Class System.

Every SS+ Rank or higher Digimon belongs to one of the following classes.

ClassRolePrimary Do
⚔️ Skill DPS (SK)Burst Damage DealerSkills
🗡️ Auto Attack DPS (AA)Sustained Damage DealerBasic Attacks
🛡️ Tank (TA)AgroSurvivability
❤️ Support (SUP)Team BufferUtility & Damage Buffs

One of the most important differences a new player should understand is that Digimon in DMW can fill different combat roles. The three main roles you should know are:

🗡️ Auto Attack DPS​

⚔️ Skill DPS​

Skill DPS focuses on dealing massive burst damage through powerful skills. SK players therefore use a different equipment setup from AA players as they progress.

🛡️ Tank​

Tanks sacrifice damage in exchange for exceptional durability.

Unlike every other class, Tanks do not gain Basic Attribute from equipment. Tank builds focus on surviving encounters and handling the defensive side of party content. Tank equipment is built differently from pure damage builds.

❤️ Support​

Image Placeholder

Replace with a Support illustration.

Support Digimon belong to either the Skill DPS or Auto Attack DPS archetype while providing team-wide buffs.

Their F3 Skill increases the damage dealt by teammates of the same DPS archetype.

Trade-Off​

✔️ Powerful team buffs

❌ Lower personal damage

Example

UlforceVeedramon Future Mode is unique.

Instead of an offensive F3, it has defensive F3 and F4 skills.

Depending on the digimon, it can be played as either:

  • ⚔️ Skill DPS
  • 🗡️ Auto Attack DPS

🔀 Hybrid Digimon​

Hybrid Digimon can be built as either:

  • ⚔️ Skill DPS
  • 🗡️ Auto Attack DPS

They cannot perform both roles at the same time.

Current Hybrid Digimon​

  • Omegamon Merciful Mode
  • ChaosDukemon / Dukemon Crimson Mode (Awaken)

Choosing Your Tamer and Starter​

If You Want to Play Skill DPS​

Recommended Tamer​

Yoshino Why? Protection is one of the best defensive Tamer Skills available early in the game and can save you from otherwise fatal mechanics. Falcomon or Gaomon

If You Want to Play Auto Attack DPS​

Recommended Tamer​

Keenan Crier Why?

His Tamer Skill Shock grants additional Critical Damage, making him the strongest early-game choice for Auto Attack players.

Recommended Partner​

Agumon or Lalamon

Why These Choices?​

Vaccine vs Data

You do not need a Data SSS Digimon for most early-game content—or even much of the currently released late-game content.

A Vaccine SSS Digimon, however, is extremely valuable throughout the game's progression and should be prioritized.
